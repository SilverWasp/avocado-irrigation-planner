Irrigation planning

Avocado Irrigation Planner

Pick any spot on the map. It pulls that location's soil and this week's real hourly forecast, and turns it into a day-by-day, hour-by-hour watering plan — liters per tree, kept inside your water budget and pump, biased to water a little less rather than risk root rot. Everything it pulls automatically, you can also override by hand.

Mode

Fixed, offline demonstration: exactly 2 hot / 1 rainy / 4 normal days, dated to this week's Sunday. Live forecast is disabled here on purpose — the brief it comes from is never meant to be silently replaced by real weather. Switch to Field planning for a real dated week with live data.

Advanced, optional

Soil-water balance (TAW / RAW)

Tracks a root-zone "bucket" of stored water from day to day — separate from, and more detailed than, the soil-type multiplier above — so the plan can show real depletion and stress risk, not just a liters total. When enabled, it already affects the schedule: it decides how much water is safe to advance onto the day before a rest day (see "Rest day" below) — it does not yet influence any other day's own scheduled amount. Needs real ET₀ for a day (Field planning mode + a live forecast pull) to simulate that day — a day without it, and every day after it this week, shows as estimated rather than fully known, since the gap itself is unmeasured. Defaults below are generic textbook midpoints (FAO-56, soil water balance), not a validated reading for your plot — replace them with your own soil test or agronomist's figures when you have them.

Plot & pump limits

A tree that hasn't filled its canopy footprint yet draws proportionally less water, independent of the land/density reference above. Picking a stage fills in an illustrative coverage (≈50% / 75% / 95%) and a matching root-zone depth for the soil-water balance below — both are editable estimates, not measured figures, so override them if you've actually measured either one. This plan models one uniform zone; if the plot has genuinely mixed ages, plan each age group separately (as its own plot/session) and total the results rather than averaging a stage here.

This is a simplified flow check (zone emitter demand vs. pump rating) — not a complete pressure-network/hydraulic design. Confirm real feasibility with whoever installs or services the system.

A day showing more applied than its own demand isn't an error: that's advance watering ahead of the rest day. The rest day's own bar shows what it would have needed, even though nothing is actually poured on it that day.
